# Documentation assistant evaluation

The final evaluation passed 8 of 12 cases, with 2 partial answers and 2 failures.
The [October baseline](2026-10-23-review.md) passed 6 of 12, with 2 partial answers
and 4 failures. RGB plotting, hypsometric tints and two-column facets improved.
The comparison of raster selection and filtering regressed.

## Method

Ran each of the 12 cases once across two batches against the public
endpoint, using the active prompt and the development documentation index.
The evaluator requested that similarity caching and query rewriting be disabled.
All requests returned HTTP 200. The follow-up received the preceding question
and answer. The [first batch](2026-11-04T21-15-15.732Z/) covered previously
problematic cases and the follow-up setup. The
[second batch](2026-11-04T21-18-36.357Z/) covered the remaining baseline passes.

Ran the returned R examples without edits in fresh R 4.6.2 processes with the
local package loaded. Supplied a three-layer `temp` raster for the two questions
that assumed it existed. Checked plot builds and verified that the mutation
example returned one raster layer with a value of 10.
